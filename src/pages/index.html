Child Care Clinic

Caring treatment for children of every age

بچوں کا توجہ اور شفقت سے علاج

From a newborn's first check-up to a school-going child's fever, our clinic gives every child calm, careful attention and gives parents clear advice they can follow at home.

Child check-ups

Consultation and examination for babies, children and teenagers, with simple explanations for parents.

Fever, cough & cold

Treatment for fever, flu, chest infections, sore throat, ear pain and other common childhood illnesses.

Diarrhoea & stomach problems

Care for loose motions, vomiting and dehydration, including ORS guidance so your child recovers quickly.

Newborn care

Advice on feeding, jaundice, cord care, sleep and the warning signs every new parent should know.

Growth & nutrition

Weight and height checks, diet advice for weak or underweight children, and support for picky eaters.

Vaccination guidance

Help keeping your child's EPI vaccination schedule on track, with reminders of which vaccine is due next.

Nebulization

Nebulizer treatment for wheezing and breathing difficulty, given at the clinic when the doctor advises it.

FAQ

Questions parents often ask

Where is Adil Child Care Clinic & Pharmacy?

We are in Rohillanwali, Tehsil and District Muzaffargarh, Punjab, Pakistan. Tap Get Directions to open the location in Google Maps.

How do I order medicines online?

Add medicines to your cart, go to checkout and tap Place order on WhatsApp. Your order opens in WhatsApp; send it and we'll confirm availability and price before you pay. You can pick it up from the shop or ask about home delivery.

Can I order medicines on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send us your medicine list or a photo of your prescription on WhatsApp. We will confirm availability and price, and keep your order ready for pickup.

Do I need a prescription to buy medicines?

Prescription-only medicines such as antibiotics are dispensed only against a valid doctor's prescription. Common over-the-counter medicines and baby care products do not need one.

Do you treat newborn babies?

Yes. Our clinic sees children of all ages, from newborns to teenagers. For a sick newborn, call us before coming so we can guide you right away.
